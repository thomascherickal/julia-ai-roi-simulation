# Comparing the 5 Forecasting Lenses & Plain-English Glossary

## Why Look at Your AI Rollout Through 5 Lenses?

Every forecasting method acts like a different camera angle on your business:
- **Lens 1 (10,000 What-If Futures)** gives the CFO an honest **Cautious (`P10`) to Best-Case (`P90`) dollar range**.
- **Lens 2 (Team Behavior)** shows HR and team leaders how **AI Champions** prevent paid licenses from turning into unused **shelfware**.
- **Lens 3 (Flywheels & Brakes)** shows the CEO how **growing team know-how** competes against **sloppy cleanup work and rising cloud bills** over 3 years.
- **Lens 4 (Workflow Waiting Lines)** shows operations leaders where **final human reviewers** will get backed up when AI speeds up first drafts.
- **Lens 5 (Pilot Scorecard)** gives the Audit & Finance Committee a clear **6-month checkpoint rule** before releasing the next stage of funding.

## Plain-English Glossary (Every Term Explained in 60 Words or Fewer)

In the web app (`index.html` and `lab.html`), every technical term or statistic is **underlined in light gold** and displays its plain-English tooltip (`<= 60 words`) on hover, focus, or tap:

- **Return on Investment (`ROI`):** How much net profit you make for every dollar spent, shown as a percentage. `+100% ROI` means you got your original investment back plus an equal amount in profit.
- **Net Profit Today (`NPV`):** Total 3-year profit minus all costs, adjusted so money earned in future years is worth slightly less than money in hand today.
- **Cautious Case (`P10`):** A conservative outcome where 90% of simulated scenarios do better than this number, and only 10% do worse. Use this to test your downside protection.
- **Typical Case (`P50` / Median):** The middle outcome across 10,000 simulated futures—half turn out better and half turn out worse. Safer for planning than an optimistic sales pitch.
- **Best Case (`P90`):** An upside outcome that only the top 10% of simulated rollouts achieve when adoption is fast and cleanup time stays low.
- **Middle 50% Range (`P25` to `P75`):** The most likely middle band of outcomes after trimming away the worst 25% and best 25% of scenarios.
- **Breakeven Month (`Payback`):** The month when total money saved finally catches up to total money spent on setup, training, and software licenses.
- **Total 3-Year Cost (`TCO`):** Everything you spend over 36 months combined—upfront IT setup, security, employee training, monthly seat fees, and AI usage bills.
- **Rollout S-Curve:** How new tools spread in real life—starting slowly with a small pilot, speeding up as word spreads, and leveling off near a natural ceiling.
- **Early Learning Dip (`J-Curve`):** The temporary slowdown in the first few months when employees spend time learning new AI habits before their speed picks up.
- **Cleanup & Fact-Checking Time (`Rework Drag`):** Hours employees spend double-checking AI drafts, fixing mistakes, or cleaning up sloppy outputs before work can ship.
- **Time-to-Profit Conversion (`Realization`):** The share of saved employee hours that actually turns into real financial value—like shipping more products, closing more sales, or avoiding extra hiring.
- **AI Usage Bill Creep (`Token Sprawl`):** When teams move from simple chat questions to automated AI assistants that run dozens of background steps, causing monthly cloud bills to climb.
- **Paid But Unused Seats (`Shelfware`):** Software licenses the company keeps paying for every month after frustrated employees quietly stop using the tool.
- **AI Champions:** Enthusiastic, well-trained teammates who coach their coworkers and help everyone get past early frustrations twice as fast.
- **Cleanup Backlog (`Technical Debt`):** Sloppy or unverified AI work that piles up over time, forcing staff to slow down later to fix mistakes.
- **Company Know-How Flywheel (`Data Moat`):** Shared prompt playbooks and company-specific workflows that make your teams faster and more accurate with AI every month.
- **Final Human Review Bottleneck (`Stage-3 QA`):** When AI helps staff draft work 30% faster, the senior managers who must review and approve that work can get overwhelmed by the waiting line.
- **Reviewer Busyness (`Utilization %`):** How much of the workday human reviewers spend checking items. Above 85% busyness, waiting lines grow dramatically.
- **On-Time Delivery Rate (`SLA`):** The percentage of customer tickets, contracts, or code changes finished within your promised turnaround time.
- **Starting Estimate (`Prior`) & Updated Forecast (`Posterior`):** Your initial Day-1 return forecast vs. your revised forecast after factoring in real 6-month pilot adoption, quality, and budget results.
- **Next-Stage Funding Decision (`Stage-Gate`):** A disciplined checkpoint at Month 6 where leaders decide to scale up (`Green Light`), fix workflow issues first (`Fix First`), or pause spending (`Hold`).
